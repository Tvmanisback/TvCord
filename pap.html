TVCORD / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Version 1.0

This document describes TvCord's current rules and intended data practices. It is not a guarantee that automated systems catch every violation. The service operator should publish a contact method and obtain appropriate legal review before broad public operation.

1. TvCord processes account identifiers needed to authenticate your account.
2. Login credentials are handled by the authentication provider; do not share passwords.
3. Your username and profile details may be visible to people using the service.
4. Profile images, banners, and accent preferences are stored to provide profile features.
5. Messages are stored so conversations can be displayed and synchronized.
6. Server membership and roles are stored to enforce access permissions.
7. Friend requests and accepted connections are stored to provide social features.
8. Files you choose to upload are stored by the configured storage provider.
9. Uploaded files may be accessible through public URLs in the current configuration.
10. Do not upload sensitive personal, financial, medical, or confidential information.
11. Technical logs may be processed by hosting and database providers for operations.
12. IP addresses and device information may be processed by infrastructure providers.
13. Data is used to operate, maintain, and troubleshoot TvCord.
14. Data may be used to detect spam, abuse, security incidents, and policy violations.
15. Data may be used to enforce service rules and protect users.
16. TvCord does not need your contacts to provide core chat functionality.
17. Do not assume messages are end-to-end encrypted; they are not currently described as such.
18. Server members with permission may view messages and files shared in their spaces.
19. People who receive public file links may be able to access those files.
20. Data may be processed by Supabase and other providers used to host the service.
21. Providers may process data in locations different from where you live.
22. Data may be disclosed when required by law or to address credible safety threats.
23. Data may be disclosed to investigate abuse, fraud, or attacks on the service.
24. TvCord does not promise that all content will be retained indefinitely.
25. Deletion from the interface may not immediately remove backups or provider logs.
26. You may request account or data deletion through the service operator's published contact method.
27. Some records may be retained when legally required or needed to resolve security issues.
28. Use a strong unique password and protect your email account.
29. Notify the service operator if you believe your account has been compromised.
30. Privacy controls and data handling may change as the service develops.
31. TvCord is not intended to collect unnecessary sensitive information from children.
32. Parents or guardians should supervise younger users' use of online communication tools.
33. Do not post another person's personal data without permission or a lawful basis.
34. Questions or privacy requests may be sent through the official contact method published with TvCord.
35. This policy may be updated; material changes should be communicated where reasonably practical.

For questions, use the official contact method published by the TvCord operator.
