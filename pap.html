Privacy Policy

Draft — add contact details, retention periods, and deletion procedures before public launch.

Information processed

TvCord uses Supabase to handle login credentials, account identifiers, usernames, profile settings, messages, server membership, friend requests, and files you choose to upload.

How information is used

Information is processed to provide chat, account access, moderation, and security.

Visibility and sharing

Messages are available to members authorized for the relevant server or conversation. Uploaded media may use public URLs, so anyone who obtains a link may be able to view that file. Avoid uploading sensitive information.

Security and retention

Reasonable access controls should be used, but no online service can guarantee perfect security. Data may remain until removed from the database and storage according to the operator's retention practices.

Your choices

Do not share information you do not want stored. A complete account export and deletion workflow must be provided by the service operator; do not assume this draft itself implements those controls.
